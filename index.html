About Us

Jimmys Coffee was established in 2021 by Eoin Duffy and Tom Duffy from Mayo, Ireland.

What began as a simple idea has grown into stores built around quality, community, and connection.

We serve great coffee, fresh food, and every visit with warmth and a smile.

Community

Proudly supporting local teams, clubs, and community moments.

Community has always been part of Jimmys. From the people we serve every day to the clubs, teams, and local groups that bring towns together, we are proud to support the places that support us.

Whether it is match day, a fundraiser, or a local partnership, Jimmys is here to back the people making good things happen.
