About Us

Jimmy's Coffee was established in 2021 by Eóin Duffy and Tom Duffy, proudly from Mayo, Ireland.

What began with a simple idea has grown into a welcoming space built around quality, community, and connection. At Jimmy's, our goal has always been clear: to serve consistently high-quality coffee, fresh food, and great produce while putting people at the heart of everything we do.

From the customers we welcome each day to the team behind every cup, we believe great coffee is about more than what's served. It's about creating moments, conversations, and experiences that keep people coming back.

Whether you're grabbing your morning coffee, meeting friends, or stopping by on the go, Jimmy's is here to serve every visit with quality, warmth, and a smile.

Our Specialty Menu

Every hot drink includes a complimentary house brownie bite on the lid! Explore our curated selections below.

Double Espresso

Two concentrated shots of our Roasted Brown single-origin espresso, rich with caramel sweetness and a golden crema.

Americano

A bold double shot of espresso extended with hot filtered water, clean and bright with a satisfying finish.

Latte

Velvety steamed milk poured over a double shot of espresso, finished with a delicate leaf of latte art.

Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, steamed milk and thick velvety foam, the perfectly balanced Italian classic.

Flat White

A double ristretto shot of Roasted Brown espresso with a smaller pour of silky microfoamed milk, concentrated and smooth.

Mocha

Espresso blended with rich Belgian chocolate, topped with silky steamed milk and a swirl of chocolate drizzle.

Hot Chocolate

Luscious Belgian hot chocolate made with real cocoa, topped with a cloud of foam and marshmallows.

Kids Hot Chocolate

A smaller, perfectly sweet hot chocolate made for little ones, topped with mini marshmallows.

Chai Latte

Aromatic spiced chai tea concentrate blended with steamed milk, warming and richly spiced.

Tea

Classic breakfast or herbal tea served in a proper cup with a saucer, steeped to perfection.

Babyccino

A tiny cup of warm velvety steamed milk foam, a beloved treat for our youngest drive-thru visitors.

Matcha

Ceremonial grade matcha whisked with your choice of steamed milk, earthy and naturally sweet.

Croissant

Classic all-butter croissant with golden flaky layers, baked fresh each morning and perfectly crisp.

Pain au Chocolat

Buttery laminated pastry wrapped around two fingers of rich Belgian dark chocolate, warm and indulgent.

Pain aux Raisins

Spiral-rolled custard pastry studded with plump raisins, glazed to a beautiful golden shine.

Pastel de Nata

Authentic Portuguese custard tart with a flaky caramelised shell and silky egg custard centre.

Cookies

Freshly baked chocolate chip cookies, chewy in the centre and golden at the edges — the ideal coffee companion.

Brownies

Fudgy dark chocolate brownies with a crackled top and dense, gooey centre.

Pork & Apple Sausage Roll

Seasoned Irish pork and sweet apple filling in golden flaky puff pastry, baked to perfection.

Mushroom & Chickpea Vegan Roll

A hearty vegan filling of earthy mushrooms and spiced chickpeas in the same beautifully flaky puff pastry casing.
